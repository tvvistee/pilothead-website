about

pilothead is a musician from wilmington, north carolina, dabbling in different genres such as indietronica and dream pop.

currently working on his debut album, pilothead has released 3 eps, those being "seasonal affective disorder", "pageflipper" and his most recent "distant".

his music can be described as both atmospheric and immersive, with the heavy use of dreamy guitar alongside electronic elements
